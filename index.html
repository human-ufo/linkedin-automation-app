LinkedIn Automation

Personal automation application that synchronizes job listings, messages and lead data between the operator's own devices using Google Drive as private storage.

  • The app accesses only the Google Drive folders it creates for synchronization.
  • All data remains under the owner's Google account. Nothing is shared with third parties.
  • The app is used exclusively by its owner; it is not a public service.
