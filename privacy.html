Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 5, 2026

This policy describes how the LinkedIn Automation application ("the app") handles user data when accessing Google services through OAuth.

1. What data the app accesses

The app requests access to Google Drive. It reads and writes only synchronization files (JSON dumps of job listings, messages and leads) inside folders created by the app in the user's own Drive.

2. How data is used

Data is transferred between devices owned and operated by the same user. The app does not process data for advertising, does not sell or share data with third parties, and does not use data for machine learning.

3. Data storage and retention

All data remains in the user's own Google account. Deleting the synchronization folders removes the data. Refresh tokens can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions.

4. Limited use

The app's use of Google Drive data complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
